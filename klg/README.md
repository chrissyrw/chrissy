# Kigali Legends (KLG)

Kigali Legends is a systemic open-world driving experience set in a living Kigali.

KLG is intentionally not designed as a conventional open-world game built around a fixed mission list. The city is the primary gameplay system: traffic, people, businesses, police, weather, districts and player behavior continuously create conditions from which gameplay emerges.

## Core experience

```text
LIVE IN KIGALI
      ↓
NOTICE SOMETHING
      ↓
MAKE A CHOICE
      ↓
CREATE A CONSEQUENCE
      ↓
THE CITY REACTS
      ↓
NEW OPPORTUNITIES APPEAR
      ↓
YOUR LEGEND CHANGES
```

## Current foundation

- Browser-based 3D runtime using Three.js
- Runtime/service orchestration foundation
- Canonical world-state snapshot
- Event-driven gameplay foundation
- Third-person player and driving systems
- Kigali-inspired world and district systems
- Vehicles, ownership, customization and garage systems
- Traffic, NPC population, police and wanted systems
- Weather and city-life simulation
- Mission and activity directors
- AI world, economy and population directors
- Economy, businesses and progression

## Design direction

The player is a **catalyst**, not a character following a checklist.

Activities should emerge from live world conditions. A traffic incident can create a shortcut opportunity. A busy market can create a delivery opportunity. Rain can change a hill route. Business demand can create work. Police pressure can alter risk. Player behavior should gradually create an identity and relationships with the city.

Read `KLG_VISION.md` for the full game-design direction.

## World identity

- **Kimironko:** commerce, crowds, deliveries and market pressure
- **Nyamirambo:** motos, dense streets, nightlife and shortcuts
- **Nyabugogo:** transit, logistics and congestion
- **CBD / Kacyiru:** business, premium movement and contracts
- **Rebero / Mount Kigali:** elevation, switchbacks and driving skill

## Production principle

KLG is being developed as a systemic game rather than a feature collection. Gameplay rules, simulation, AI orchestration and content are being separated from engine-specific presentation wherever practical so the project can grow toward a full production pipeline.
